# 2020 Tieba Post

The type is a post on 街机吧 (the arcade forum) on Baidu Tieba. The URL is https://tieba.baidu.com/p/6671136635 , the title is "给大家分享一个PC上的模拟器前端。", and the post date is May 10, 2020. Reliability is personal. On opening the page, only the poster's first 2 posts could be read.

## Contents

The poster writes that they had many game ROMs on their PC and wanted to play whenever they felt like it, but opening an emulator and finding the ROM was a hassle. They looked for a way to double-click a list and launch automatically, and they bought 月光宝盒, 风暴英雄2000, a set-top box, and a Raspberry Pi, but none of them supported as many games as a PC. That was when they found this frontend.

The poster says they cannot upload the pack to 百度网盘 and that packaging it is a hassle, so they advise searching for "天马模拟器前端" and downloading the 15GB pack. They note that the pack does not contain every game. The frontend itself was developed overseas, and what the poster used was a domestic 整合包. They announce that they will begin explaining a simple way to add games, but within what could be retrieved, the continuation could not be read.

This shows that as of 2020 the Tianma frontend was circulating as a domestic 整合包 and that the search name was "天马模拟器前端".
