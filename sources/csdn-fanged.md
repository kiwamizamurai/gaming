# CSDN Article on Tianma G

The type is a personal blog on CSDN. The URL is https://blog.csdn.net/fanged/article/details/152960565 , the title is "天马G前端的使用", and the article is dated October 2025. Reliability is personal, and it describes steps the author tried in their own environment. The body could not be read right after opening the page, so it was read after waiting a little.

## Contents

The author had an unused 小米9 (Snapdragon 855) and had also obtained a controller, so began writing with the aim of building a retro device. First, the author divides the handhelds on the market into two types, Android and Linux, and compares them in a table. Android takes 20 to 40 seconds to boot and has a different screen for each app. PS2 runs on AetherSX2, there are also unofficial versions for Switch, and cloud gaming and Steam Link are supported. Linux systems (ArkOS, JELOS, Batocera) boot within 5 seconds, have a unified interface, and allow settings to be changed freely, but require manual setup of BIOS files and cores.

The author explains that PS2 emulators lean toward Android because their creator, Tahlreth, made AetherSX2 only for Android and implemented JIT with the Android NDK and Vulkan/OpenGL ES. JIT is a technique that converts the instructions of the original console into the instructions of the running machine during execution and optimizes "hotspots".

As devices on the market, the author writes that 安伯尼克's RG35XX has a T700 (1×2.5GHz A76 and 3×2.3GHz A76) and the RG406V a T820 (1×2.7GHz A76 and 3×2.3GHz A76), and that the price rose 2 to 3 times despite no great increase in performance. However, the statement that the RG35XX has a T700 conflicts with ROCKNIX's documents, which classify the RG35XX as an H700 device. The statement that the RG406V's T820 has 4 cores also differs from the 8 cores listed on 掌机圈. The figures may be the author's errors, so they cannot be used as they stand. The author notes that their 小米9 is far above the T820 in both CPU and GPU and cost about 350 yuan used, calculates that even with a controller added it comes to half the price of an RG406V, and decides to build their own.

Installation has three stages: APKs, settings, and ROMs. The APKs are Tianma G itself, RetroArch, the various emulators, and the file manager MT管理器, which the author installed with adb. The settings are three items: the Tianma G theme pack, the configuration files, and the `Android` folder. On Android 11 and later, each app's data folder is protected, and because Tianma G shares settings and ROM paths among Pegasus, RetroArch, and AetherSX2, manual placement in advance is required. For ROMs, a main image, secondary image, introduction video, and metadata are needed in addition to the ROM files, and in the "跳坑者联盟" pack they are provided inside the 精簡包. The author writes that ROMs are a sensitive matter, so emulator creators and studios do not provide them, and that within China the "跳坑者联盟" pack circulates widely.

When actually run, the author says, the interface was flashy and a USB-connected controller had almost no latency. On the other hand, because of the shape of the smartphone screen, 4:3 games get large black bars on the left and right. The author writes that PSP looks good.

The last part explains the technical principle of Tianma G. Tianma G is merely a frontend, responsible for managing ROMs and launching emulators. The actual processing is left to each emulator's APK. The official site is https://pegasus-frontend.org/ and it is made with Qt. The core code on Android is a function called `launchAmCommand`. This function assembles an `am start` Intent and calls `startActivity` through JNI to launch a third-party emulator. The author writes that you can also play by installing RetroArch directly, which can sometimes be simpler. The final section, "FPGA extensions", is a future interest in whether emulator performance can be improved with FPGAs.
