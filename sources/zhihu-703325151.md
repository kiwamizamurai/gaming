# Zhihu article introducing Tianma G

The type is a 什么值得买 article reposted on Zhihu. The URL is https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/703325151 , the title is "模拟器游戏 篇四:神器降临!这可能是PC最强的万合一模拟器,无比强大!", and the post date is June 14, 2024. Reliability is personal (reposted), and the body is an account of the author's experience trying it on their own PC. Opening the page overlays a screen prompting login, so the body was read from the page text.

## Contents

The author introduces Tianma G as an emulator frontend running on mobile and PC that combines the overseas open source Pegasus with 汉化 (Chinese localization) by the domestic "跳坑者联盟". It bundles core files such as RA, PPSSPP, RetroArch, and Dolphin, and can handle dozens of systems. "跳坑者联盟" also makes 汉化 game packs, including GBA, NDS, 3DS, PSP, PS1, PS2, Wii, and arcade, totaling about 2.8TB. The contents can be chosen to suit storage capacity, and what this article recommended was a 220GB 精简版 that the author had slimmed down further. Each game has a cover, name, and introduction video, and 8 themes are built in. The author writes that on 闲鱼 there are 商家 selling this for around 20 yuan.

The author gives three steps for installing on a PC. First, install the emulator itself. Choose the archive suited to your environment from "安装包" and extract it to the root of a drive. A folder called "Pegasus G" is created, and the author cautions that the emulator will not work correctly if it is placed anywhere other than the root. The author also says that it will always crash unless the necessary video plugins are installed. Next, place the game ROMs. Extract the contents of the 精简包 into `roms` under "Pegasus G". File names must not be changed. Finally, launch the Tianma G launcher and play.

On controls and dealing with problems, the author writes that keys differ by emulator, that in PPSSPP Esc opens settings and pressing it twice quits, and that the space key is fast-forward and Enter is launch. A problem with no sound was fixed with 3DM's game runtime. A crash on Wii was fixed by installing all of the optional drivers in "解决闪退工具". The author tried it on Android 14 (澎湃OS), and because some emulators showed as unsupported, speculates that Tianma G was made for open source Android 掌机.
